KKodrat · Solution 02 / 08
Kodrat — Self-serve FX insurance

Pick your exposure, pay a premium,
get paid if FX moves against you.

Short-tenor FX options, quoted by market makers and cash-settled in USDC.

01 / Buyer

Hedge like a treasury,
without becoming one.

Choose exporter or importer, pair, amount, and tenor. Kodrat translates the business problem into the right option and shows premium, strike, expiry, and payout scenarios.

→ User risk is limited to the premium paid.
02 / Market Maker

Quote risk off-chain,
settle trustlessly.

MMs register through an MmRegistry PDA and sign RFQ quotes off-chain. The program verifies the signature before accepting a trade.

→ Day-1: one MM. v2: competitive marketplace.
03 / Settlement

Transparent collateral,
automatic payout.

Premiums and collateral sit in an MM USDC vault. At expiry, anyone can settle; the program reads the price feed and pays intrinsic value to the buyer.

→ USDC payout offsets the FX loss.

# On-chain (settlement)

KKodrat · Why Solana 05 / 08
Timing & Infrastructure

SME hedges only work when small tickets
are economically worth serving.

Cost per settlement (open + close)

Solana Kodrat ~$0.001
Ethereum L2 (Base) ~$0.05
Ethereum L1 ~$5–50
Bank wire (BCA) $15 + 0.25%

For a USD 50 premium, high transaction fees erase the product. Low fees let the minimum ticket fall to real SME order sizes.

Why now

~18M Crypto users in Indonesia have normalized wallet-based financial access.
Trust gap The viral Google rate glitch showed how quickly bad FX information can move through WhatsApp and trading decisions.
64M Indonesian SMEs face FX exposure without derivatives access.
Zero Self-serve FX options for SME ticket sizes are still missing.
Solana gives Kodrat the cost profile of software, not banking infrastructure: small hedges, fast settlement, transparent collateral.
KKodrat · Why RFQ 06 / 08
Tech Feasibility

RFQ turns derivatives into a marketplace,
not a balance-sheet monopoly.

Quote latency · end-to-end RFQ

Solana Kodrat ~1.2s
Ethereum L2 (Base) ~4–8s
Ethereum L1 ~30–60s

Options quotes expire quickly because spot and volatility move. Solana keeps quote acceptance inside the useful window.

Cost per accept / failed retry

Solana Kodrat $0.001
Ethereum L2 (Base) $0.05
Ethereum L1 $5–50

Cheap retries allow tight spreads and a beginner-friendly UX: refresh quote, accept, settle, repeat.
